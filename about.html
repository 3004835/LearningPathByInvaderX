OUR PURPOSE

A welcoming place to keep learning

School learning is full of small moments: a fraction finally makes sense, a science question leads to another, or a sentence becomes clearer after a second try. LearnifyHub brings approachable explanations and practical activities together so students can explore those moments at their own pace.

From mathematics and science to English, typing, and study tools, LearnifyHub brings helpful learning resources together in one convenient place. You can move between subjects whenever your curiosity takes you there.

What you can do here

02 / PRACTICE

Designed around access

We believe education should be accessible to everyone. That's why LearnifyHub is designed to let students start learning without creating an account or paying a subscription. There is no account setup standing between you and the learning activities.

Practice scores shown on the site are there to guide the current activity. They are not a permanent student record, and you can simply begin again whenever you return.

Learning with care

Learning materials are most useful when they are clear, respectful, and honest about what they cover. We aim to explain ideas in student-friendly language, show the reasoning behind examples, and keep activities focused on skill building. A short activity can be a helpful start, while a teacher, textbook, or course may provide additional context and depth.

LearnifyHub is an independent learning resource. We do not claim school partnerships or endorsements. If a collaboration is established in the future, it should be described accurately and with the partner's permission.

Frequently asked questions

Is LearnifyHub free to use?

Yes. The learning activities on this site are available without a subscription or paywall.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can open a subject and begin using its available lessons and activities right away.

Does my progress get saved?

Practice feedback and progress indicators currently operate in your browser while you use the activity. They are not tied to an account or saved as a long-term profile.

Who is LearnifyHub for?

It is made for students and anyone who wants a straightforward way to review foundational topics. Choose material at a comfortable level and ask a teacher for course-specific guidance when needed.

Can I suggest a subject or report a correction?

Yes. Send a note through the contact page. Include the page or topic and a brief description so the team can understand your suggestion.

Can teachers use these materials?

Teachers and families can explore the public activities as supplementary practice. Please review material before using it in a lesson and follow any applicable copyright notices on linked resources.
