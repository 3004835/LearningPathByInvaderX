Understanding fractions

A fraction represents equal parts of a whole. Its numerator (top number) counts the parts you have; its denominator (bottom number) tells how many equal parts make the whole.

EXAMPLE3/4 means 3 of 4 equal parts.

A / LIFE SCIENCE

Living things

Cells are the smallest units that can carry out the processes of life. Some organisms have one cell; plants and animals are made of many specialized cells.

B / CHEMISTRY

Matter & atoms

Everything you can touch is made of matter. Matter is built from atoms, which can join in different combinations to form molecules.

C / PHYSICS

Forces & motion

A force is a push or a pull. Unbalanced forces change an object's motion; balanced forces leave its motion unchanged.

Our solar system

Eight planets orbit the Sun. Their paths are held by gravity, the attractive force between objects with mass.

03 — English & Reading

Find the right
words for it.

Language is a tool for thinking and sharing. Strengthen the skills that help you read closely and write clearly.

01 / GRAMMAR

Build a clear sentence

A complete sentence needs a subject and a predicate. The subject tells who or what the sentence is about; the predicate tells what the subject does or is.

The curious student asked a thoughtful question.
02 / VOCABULARY

Learn words in context

Context clues are hints around an unfamiliar word. Look for examples, contrasts, or explanations nearby before reaching for a dictionary.

The path was steep; we climbed it at a sharp angle. “Sharp angle” helps explain steep.

04 — Typing practice

Build a rhythm.
Find your flow.

Begin with the home row. Rest your fingers lightly on A, S, D, F and J, K, L, ;. The raised marks on F and J help you find the keys without looking.
