BEFORE YOU WRITE

Quick answers

Can I ask for help with a school assignment?

We can consider general questions or suggestions about learning topics on the site. Include the subject and concept so we can understand what resource would be useful.

What if I find a mistake?

Choose “Correction or content feedback” and mention the exact lesson, sentence, or question. Specific context makes it easier to review.

How do I suggest a new topic?

Choose “New subject or activity idea” and describe the skill or idea a learner should practice. Suggestions help us understand what students want to explore.

Will I get a reply?

If you would like a reply, include a reachable email address in the form or send from your email account. We cannot promise a response time.
