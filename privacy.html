Privacy

LearnifyHub is designed so students can use its learning activities without creating an account. The math practice, flashcards, quizzes, and typing activity on this site run in your browser. This site does not ask students to submit a name, email address, or other personal details to use these activities.

Information and browser storage

The current site does not use accounts or store practice progress on a server. Your answers and typing activity are used in the page to provide feedback and are not saved when you leave or refresh it. Your browser or hosting provider may process standard technical information when loading a website. If this site later adds analytics, embedded services, or other data collection, this notice should be updated to explain those practices before they are enabled.

Links and contact

Links to other sites are governed by those sites' own privacy practices. For questions about this notice, contact hello@learnifyhub.us.
